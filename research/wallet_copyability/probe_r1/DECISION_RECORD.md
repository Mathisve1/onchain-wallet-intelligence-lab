8. **Status is derived.** Human prose or workflow conclusion cannot override observations.
9. **Capacity is preregistered.** Base declared weight is 586; two-attempt worst case is 1,172 under the captured 1,200 planning budget.
10. **Artifact before terminal failure.** Evidence is uploaded even when validation fails.
11. **Attestation is separate.** The read-only workflow cannot self-attest; later review binds exact identities.
12. **Rejected predecessors remain negative evidence.** PR #1–#3 cannot be cited as successful R1 runs.
13. **Human-in-the-loop remains binding.** No account action or trading authorization exists.
14. **Next gate is not automatic.** An attested PASS permits only a separately reviewed inventory proposal.
