# Entity Clustering Boundary

## First-study confirmed links

The individual copyability study may consolidate only relationships explicitly
available from the protocol or an approved public service-role label:

- master/subaccount;
- official address role;
- official vault relationship;
- known exchange, bridge, vault or protocol service node.

Confirmed linked wallets count as one independence vote, while gross and net
exposure remain visible.

## Successor probabilistic links

After individual copyability passes, a separate study may consider:

- recurring direct transfers;
- common treasury or beneficiary flows;
- synchronized trading with stable delay;
- identical unusual order-size ladders;
- common funding sources;
- execution-engine fingerprints.

These are hypotheses about economic or operational linkage, not automatic claims
that wallets belong to one named person.

## Prohibited merge behaviour

- no merge from a single transfer;
- no merge because two users share an exchange or bridge;
- no weak transitive closure;
- no future evidence applied retroactively;
- no LLM-only merge;
- no real-world identity claim without lawful independent public evidence.

Every edge has `first_observed_at`, `evidence_available_at`, confidence, edge
type, model version and supporting event hashes. Cluster versions are immutable
and point-in-time reproducible.
