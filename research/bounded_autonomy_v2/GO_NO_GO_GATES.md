# Promotion gates — DEX-COPY-AUTONOMY-001

No gate automatically authorizes the next stage. A gate may make the next proposal admissible; the human governor must still explicitly promote it.

## A0 — lane isolation

PASS requires:

- autonomy work lives only in its isolated directory/branch while the parallel data lane is active;
- no canonical probe evidence, attestation, archive canary or parent result is modified;
- no repository-level status promotion is claimed;
- current execution/capital authorizations remain false.

Failure means stop and reconcile branches before any further write.

## A1 — design-contract completeness

PASS requires:

- closed AI/human/deterministic authority model;
- closed action allowlist;
- typed intent schema;
- typed one-time permit schema;
- human-frozen risk-envelope schema with no hidden defaults;
- signer/nonce/cloid design;
- kill-switch/recovery state machine;
- latency contract;
- threat model and adversarial matrix;
- official-source register;
- no contradictory permissions across documents.

## A2 — pure deterministic implementation

Future implementation, no venue actions.

PASS requires:

- schema validation;
- deterministic canonicalization and SHA-256 hashing;
- closed action compiler;
- policy evaluator;
- risk-envelope evaluator;
- permit issuance/consume store;
- mock signer that cannot sign arbitrary bytes;
- nonce/cloid allocator abstraction;
- reconciliation state machine;
- all adversarial tests that require no network;
- two byte-identical results with frozen time/nonce fixtures.

Any arbitrary-action escape is critical no-go.

## A3 — parent follower-alpha evidence

Owned by the separate data/research lane.

Autonomy research remains economically blocked until the parent study has point-in-time source signals and demonstrates that follower alpha exists after costs under the preregistered research rules.

Minimum future conditions include:

- chronological out-of-sample evaluation;
- positive net follower expectancy;
- conservative lower confidence bound positive under registered primary analysis;
- p90 latency stress and doubled-cost stress;
- concentration/capacity checks;
- source returns and follower returns separated.

If follower alpha fails, faster execution cannot rescue the strategy and the execution lane must return `NO_GO_NO_COPYABLE_ALPHA`.

## A4 — deterministic autonomy incremental value

Using the exact same parent signal set:

- deterministic autonomy must be compared with measured human one-click/manual modes;
- blocked/missed/expired signals stay in the denominator;
- p50/p75/p90/p95 latency is measured end-to-end;
- improvement must survive conservative costs;
- tail loss and drawdown may not materially worsen beyond frozen limits.

A faster mean with worse integrity/tail risk is not a pass.

## A5 — AI critic incremental value

The AI critic is evaluated only after deterministic autonomy is a valid baseline.

PASS requires:

- AI cannot expand eligibility/size/risk;
- AI timeouts/errors are explicitly counted;
- registered trial family/correction is applied to multiple AI variants;
- incremental economic value is positive out of sample;
- security/latency cost of AI is included;
- no material deterioration in missed-signal or tail-loss behavior.

If AI adds no value, bounded autonomy may continue without AI on the execution critical path.

## A6 — non-signing shadow

Requires separate human promotion.

Minimum future evidence:

- at least 90 calendar days;
- at least 500 eligible events after point-in-time filters, adjusted for dependence;
- component latency distribution and queue-health metrics;
- full hypothetical intent -> policy -> permit -> action -> reconciliation lineage;
- zero unexplained state transitions;
- stale/conflict/outage cases fail closed;
- shadow economics directionally consistent with historical estimates.

No signature or venue action exists in this stage.

## A7 — signer/policy adversarial proof

Before any signed stage:

- exact signer, adapter and policy versions frozen;
- zero successful unauthorized signatures/actions across critical matrix;
- replay, mutation, expiry and duplicate tests pass;
- nonce and cloid non-reuse proven under concurrency/restart;
- AI compromise cannot reach key material or raw action endpoint;
- independent human kill/revoke path tested;
- recovery drill completed;
- no secret material in logs/artifacts/model context.

One unauthorized signature is immediate no-go.

## A8 — signed testnet

Requires a disposable test identity and separate human authorization.

PASS requires:

- no real capital;
- exact IOC/cancel/reduce-only actions only;
- acknowledgement/order/fill/position reconciliation;
- partial fill/reject/expiry/reconnect/duplicate flows;
- HTTP versus WebSocket submission benchmarking;
- exact rate-limit/nonce behavior captured;
- no orphan order or unexplained position;
- emergency pause/cancel/recovery drills.

## A9 — isolated live canary

Requires a new explicit human decision and a fully populated risk envelope.

Conditions:

- one isolated account/subaccount;
- one dedicated API wallet;
- canary capital whose total loss is explicitly accepted by the owner;
- no client funds;
- no withdrawal/transfer path in the strategy adapter;
- no leverage/margin changes by AI;
- automatic pause on integrity/reconciliation failure;
- execution correctness evaluated before profitability;
- frequent human audit review.

A profitable canary does not authorize scale.

## A10 — scale proposal

Requires a separately frozen period, stable live execution, capacity evidence, no unresolved incident, independent security/model review and a new human-frozen capital/risk envelope.

The AI cannot initiate or approve scale.
